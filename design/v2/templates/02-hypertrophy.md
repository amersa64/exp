# Template 02 — Hypertrophy

The "build muscle" goal. For the user who wants to look bigger, fill out a t-shirt, have visible arms.

## Who picks this

The user who said *"I want to look better. I want to be bigger."* Concretely:

- Cares about how they look more than how much they lift
- Wants to see and feel pumps
- Wants chest, arms, shoulders, glutes to grow
- Willing to train more frequently (4-6 days)
- Comes from gym culture (YouTube, bodybuilding subreddits, Renaissance Periodization)
- Tyler from the persona walkthroughs is the prototypical user

Anti-personas:
- Wants to PR squat → Strength (01)
- Wants 30-min sessions → wrong template (hypertrophy needs volume → time)
- Wants minimal equipment → Conditioning or Longevity

## Split structure

**Two options based on days/week.**

### Upper / Lower (4 days)

| Day | Workout | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Upper A | Bench, OHP, row, lat pulldown, biceps, triceps |
| Tue | Lower A | Squat, leg press, leg curl, calves, abs |
| Thu | Upper B | Incline bench, dumbbell row, dumbbell press, biceps variation, triceps variation, rear delts |
| Fri | Lower B | Romanian deadlift, walking lunge, leg extension, calves, abs |

Wed and weekend are rest. Each muscle group hit twice per week — the consensus minimum for optimal hypertrophy frequency.

### Push / Pull / Legs (6 days)

| Day | Workout | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Push A | Bench, OHP, incline DB, lateral raises, triceps |
| Tue | Pull A | Pull-up, barbell row, face pull, biceps |
| Wed | Legs A | Squat, leg press, leg curl, calves |
| Thu | Push B | Incline bench, DB shoulder press, cable fly, lateral raises, triceps |
| Fri | Pull B | Deadlift, lat pulldown, DB row, biceps |
| Sat | Legs B | Front squat, RDL, walking lunge, calves, abs |

Sunday rest. Each muscle group hit twice per week (push/pull/legs × 2 = 6).

## Rep ranges

Hypertrophy zone with strength accents:

- Main compound lifts: **5-8 reps**, 3-4 sets
- Secondary compounds: **8-12 reps**, 3-4 sets
- Isolation: **10-15 reps**, 3 sets
- Calves and abs: **12-20 reps**, 3 sets

Variety across rep ranges drives different adaptations (myofibrillar vs sarcoplasmic hypertrophy). Strength template is mono-rep-range; hypertrophy is multi.

## Progression

**Double progression** (rep range, then weight):

For an exercise prescribed as "3 sets of 8-12 reps":
- Hit all 3 sets at 12 reps with good form → next session, add weight (small jump), reset to 8 reps
- Hit 3 sets but only 9-10 reps each → repeat, same load
- Failed below 8 → reduce load

Different from strength's linear add-weight-every-session because:
- Hypertrophy progression is *slower* (the goal is volume accumulation, not max strength)
- Failure is a recoverable event, not a deload trigger
- Weight jumps are smaller (~2.5-5 lb upper, ~5 lb lower)

After 4-6 weeks: deload week (1 set at 50% volume per exercise). Hypertrophy users need scheduled deloads because the cumulative fatigue is real — you can't just push linearly like a novice.

## Days/week supported

- 3 days → suboptimal for hypertrophy. **Recommend strength template instead**, or upper/lower truncated.
- 4 days → Upper/Lower (default)
- 5 days → Upper/Lower + 1 weak-point day (user picks: arms, shoulders, or glutes)
- 6 days → PPL
- 7 days → discouraged; recovery suffers. Cap at 6.

## Time per session

- Default: **60 min**
- Compressed (45 min): drop one isolation exercise, tighter rest (60s vs 120s for isolation)
- Extended (75 min): add one more isolation exercise per muscle group

Hypertrophy is **volume-bound**, not time-bound — but volume requires time. Below 45 min the volume gets clipped enough to dilute the goal.

## Equipment branches

This template benefits massively from a full gym:

| Equipment | What changes |
|---|---|
| Full gym | Canonical — cable machines, plate-loaded leg press, lat pulldown |
| Barbell+rack only | Drops cable work; substitutes barbell/DB isolation (DB curl, DB lateral raise, skull crusher with EZ bar) |
| Dumbbells only | Big compromise — substitutes everything to DB versions. Loses lat pulldown, leg press. Works but reduces ceiling. **User should consider gym membership for this goal.** |
| Bodyweight only | **Discourage this template entirely.** Bodyweight hypertrophy is possible (calisthenics) but very different programming. v2.1: add a `02b-calisthenics-hypertrophy.md` template if demand exists. |

## Focus muscle groups (intake conditional)

Hypertrophy uniquely asks this question during intake (other templates skip it):

> *"Which muscle groups do you most want to grow? Pick up to 3."*
>
> ◯ Chest
> ◯ Back
> ◯ Shoulders
> ◯ Arms
> ◯ Glutes
> ◯ Legs
> ◯ Core/abs

The selected groups get **+1 isolation exercise per week** dedicated to them. The user who picks "chest + arms" gets an extra chest exercise on push days and an extra arms exercise on push days. The user who picks "glutes" gets an extra hip thrust variant on leg days.

This is the closest thing to user-driven programming customization in any template. It exists for hypertrophy because the goal is *aesthetic* — and aesthetics are personal.

## Milestones

1. **First 5-lb progression on bench** — proves the double-progression system is working
2. **Hit all 3 sets at top of rep range** — first time you complete a "12-12-12" — the moment that means weight goes up next time
3. **4 weeks no missed sessions** — consistency milestone (same as strength)
4. **First photo comparison** — 8 weeks in, side-by-side with intake photo (requires photo block enabled)
5. **Squat or bench bodyweight × 8** — body-relative strength as a hypertrophy marker

## Adaptation rules

| Report | Brain action |
|---|---|
| Done, top of rep range | Add weight next session, reset to bottom of range |
| Done, mid rep range | Same load, push to top next time |
| Done, bottom of rep range | Same load, hold |
| Partial | Same load, note friction |
| Skipped | Same load, repeat session next attempt |
| 4 weeks straight no deload | Schedule deload week automatically |

## HealthKit usage (basic per user's scope)

- **Sleep:** if avg of last 3 nights <6 hours, reduce volume by 20% (drop 1 set per exercise) for the next session
- **Weight:** hypertrophy users care about body weight as a *gain* signal (if cutting) or *recomp* signal. Show trend on Becoming tab. Coach references it: *"up 2 lb in 6 weeks. Muscle's slow. Stay the course."*

## Photo block

Optional but recommended for hypertrophy users. They'll *want* progress photos. Surface during intake:

> *"Most muscle gain isn't visible week to week. Photos catch what the mirror doesn't. Want to commit to one a week?"*

If yes → Photo block appears on Coach tab one day per week. Notification at 8am: *"Weekly photo. Same time, same light."* User uploads. The Becoming tab gets a photo strip showing the progression.

Photos are private by default. Sharing requires explicit per-photo action.

## Voice register tuning

Marcus base register, with slight variations:

- After a "top of range" PR: *"all three sets at 12. weight goes up Thursday."* (specific, declarative)
- After a missed isolation set (e.g. failed curls at 10 reps in set 3): *"happens. arms were cooked. same load Thursday."* (forgiving — isolation is harder to recover from than compound progression)
- The phrase "fill out a t-shirt" is allowed in voice for this template (the Tyler vocabulary). Reads as "in Marcus's gym voice" for an aesthetics-focused lifter.

## What this template is NOT for

- People who want explicit PR numbers (route to Strength)
- People with <3 days/week to train (cut volume too much; route to a shorter-frequency template)
- People doing it for weight loss (route to Conditioning — hypertrophy maintains weight or grows it, doesn't shed it)

## Open questions for this template

- **Exercise variation cadence.** Hypertrophy benefits from rotating exercises every 4-6 weeks to avoid stagnation. Should the program auto-rotate (e.g. flat bench → incline bench at week 5) or stay constant until user complains? Probably auto-rotate every 6 weeks with a notification.
- **Rep tempo / time under tension.** Real hypertrophy programs prescribe tempo (e.g. 3-second eccentric). Do we surface this? Probably not in v2 — adds complexity. v2.1 maybe.
- **Pump-chasing accessory load.** The "burnout set" at the end of a hypertrophy workout is a thing. Do we prescribe one? Probably no in v2 — adds complexity, hard to program consistently.
- **Cutting vs bulking awareness.** Hypertrophy assumes maintenance or surplus calories. If user is cutting (Conditioning template would catch this), they shouldn't run this template. Hard product question: should we ask "are you eating to grow?" during intake? Probably yes — and route to Conditioning if they say no.
